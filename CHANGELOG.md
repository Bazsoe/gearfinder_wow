# Gearfinder – changelog

Write the newest changes at the top before publishing a release; this text appears on CurseForge.

## v2.14.2
- Detects a new WoW Forever client build (e.g. 70938 → 71091), says so in chat and re-reads the item data automatically.
- The Overview shows which client build the data was read on.

## v2.14.1
- The Overview panel no longer shows the client details (use /gf info).

## v2.14.0
- Raids follow WoW Forever: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9. Molten Core, Blackwing Lair,
  Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas are not in Forever yet, so their loot is not recommended.
- Full loot tables with item IDs for Krol'dok Stronghold and City of Dalaran; all nine new Forever dungeons are covered.
- New logo (rune medallion with a G and f monogram), golden by default, arcane blue selectable: Shift-click the minimap button,
  click the window logo, or /gf logo 1 | 2.
